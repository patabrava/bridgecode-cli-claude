# Writing — voice, reader progression, and fidelity

Use for substantial generation, editing, explanation, or narrative. The core supplies the permanent writing floor.

Two jobs need different freedoms. **Editing someone's text:** preserve their genre, voice, terminology, factual strength, attribution, and required form; your freedom is in clarity, order, and rhythm within that voice, and a mechanical correction needs no creative search. **Creating new text:** you have real creative latitude — voice, form, structure, imagery, pacing, humor or gravity — within the brief's facts and requirements. Use it.

Establish what the reader must understand, feel, decide, or do; the subject's actual facts and vocabulary; the intended experience; fixed requirements; open choices; and the boundary of done. When framing, voice, structure, emphasis, rhythm, or omission materially affects success, read `.claude/bridgecode/taste.md` and use it to widen the options and commit to a direction. A technical explanation can need taste too, where its progression determines comprehension.

## Draft

Draft with momentum toward the strongest version you can see, not the safest. Find what makes this piece specific: the concrete example, the real tension, the detail only this subject has. Let form follow the material — an essay can argue, a story can withhold, an explanation can build from a single case, reference documentation can simply be reference. Prose develops explanations and arguments; lists, tables, headings, and labels carry genuine sequences, comparisons, and reference information. Use the natural number of parts and give unequal points unequal weight. Produce the deliverable; a direction statement is not a substitute for finished writing.

## Revise

Then read the draft as its audience, from their knowledge state:

- Prerequisites come before what depends on them, and orientation and connective reasoning are present.
- Claims match evidence and uncertainty stays visible; nothing is invented to sound specific.
- Rhetorical intensity follows actual importance; agreement, praise, balance, and qualification each have a reason.
- Exact technical terms stay, explained at the reader's level rather than swapped for impressive synonyms.
- Actors, conditions, actions, consequences, ownership, and limits are stated where the reader must operate or decide.
- Repetition that obscures progression is cut, while necessary context and expressive range stay.

Fix framing and information order before polishing words. Familiar phrasing is a reason to check its fit, not proof of poor writing or AI authorship, and there is no universal blacklist of words, structures, people, or genres. Watch for combined failures that survive surface edits — prestige language with unsupported claims, exhaustive coverage with no priorities, forced informality with decorative novelty — and repair the decision behind them. Anticipate only objections the brief supports, and distinguish proposed, applied, and verified changes.

## Accept

Accept when the text serves its evidenced reader and purpose, preserves required meaning and form, grounds consequential claims, develops relationships clearly, carries a voice that fits, and stops at the requested boundary. Source or factual checks replace runtime testing for prose-only work; implemented artifacts follow the core's review contract. Report validation limits truthfully.
